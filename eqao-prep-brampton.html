A Structured Prep Program — Not Just Worksheets

Every spring, Brampton students in Grades 3, 6, and 9 sit down to write their EQAO assessments — and every spring, their parents wish the preparation had started earlier. EQAO doesn't have to be a source of stress. With an honest assessment of where your child stands, a plan that targets the actual curriculum expectations being tested, and practice in the EQAO format itself, students walk in calm and ready. That is exactly what our EQAO prep program is built to do.

1. What our EQAO prep program covers

Our program is built around what EQAO actually assesses. For Grade 3 and Grade 6 students, that means all three strands: reading, writing, and mathematics, drawn straight from the Ontario curriculum. For Grade 9 students, it is the EQAO mathematics assessment. We don't hand your child a stack of generic worksheets. An Ontario-certified teacher works through where your child stands in each strand, then targets the specific expectations EQAO will test — the reading comprehension strategies, the writing organization, the math problem-solving and communication. Every practice set we use is in the EQAO style, so the format itself is familiar long before test day: the question wording, the amount of writing expected, and the way answers need to show thinking.

2. How it works: from free trial to test day

It starts with a free trial session that doubles as an assessment. Your child's teacher identifies strengths and gaps against the EQAO expectations — not just a mark, but a clear picture of which reading, writing, and math skills are test-ready and which need work. From that assessment we build a personalized prep plan with a realistic weekly rhythm. Sessions focus on three things: filling the curriculum gaps, practising EQAO-style questions, and learning test craft — reading questions carefully, showing work clearly in math, organizing written answers, and managing time so nothing is left blank. Along the way we check progress against the plan and adjust. By the final weeks, your child has worked through enough EQAO-style material that the real assessment feels like just another practice day — which is exactly the point.

3. When to start: the 8-to-12-week window

The most common mistake parents make with EQAO is starting too late. A week of cramming cannot rebuild a shaky understanding of fractions or teach reading strategies that take time to sink in. We recommend starting 8 to 12 weeks before the spring assessment window — enough runway to fix real gaps and build genuine confidence, without turning the season into a pressure cooker. Starting earlier is always fine; with only a couple of weeks to go we can still help with test strategies and a focused review, but the deep work needs time. If you are reading this in the fall or winter, you are actually in the ideal position: steady weekly sessions now mean your child arrives at EQAO season already prepared.

4. One-on-one or small group for test prep?

Both work — it depends on your child. One-on-one prep ($40/hour) is the fastest route when there are specific gaps to close, or when your child is anxious about the test and needs a calm, private space to build confidence. Small-group prep ($25/hour per student, maximum four students) suits students who are roughly on track and want structured practice alongside peers — many students genuinely enjoy the shared energy of preparing together. Not sure which fits? The free trial assessment will tell us, and we will give you an honest recommendation.

5. Pricing built for prep season

Our twice-weekly monthly packages were practically designed for test prep: eight sessions a month at $300 for one-on-one or $180 for group — $20 less per month than pay-as-you-go. That rhythm keeps skills fresh and momentum steady through the prep window. There are no registration fees and no long-term contracts. Book the free trial, see the assessment, and decide from there.

6. For Brampton families, online and in person

We work with families across Brampton — Springdale, Castlemore, Heart Lake, Bramalea, Mount Pleasant, Credit Valley, and Fletcher's Meadow. Choose in-person sessions or join online with the same Ontario-certified teachers; many families do a mix as the test date approaches and schedules get busy.

EQAO rewards the prepared, not the panicked. Give your child the runway to prepare properly — and give yourself the peace of mind that comes with a real plan.
