Free Math Diagnostic Tests — Grades 5 to 8

Wondering where your child really stands in math? Each diagnostic is built by Ontario-certified teachers and matched to the Ontario curriculum. Print it out, have your child work through it without help, then check the parent answer key to spot the gaps — before report cards do.
